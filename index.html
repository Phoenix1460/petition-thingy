    The ask

    We’re asking our school to offer biodegradable utensils in the cafeteria instead of disposable plastic ones. It’s a simple, practical change—and every signature helps show that students care about a lighter, cleaner lunch culture.

    Why this matters

    A greener cafeteria starts with a simple switch.

    Plastic utensils are convenient, but they’re also wasteful. By choosing biodegradable options, we can reduce unnecessary trash while showing our school that thoughtful daily habits matter.
